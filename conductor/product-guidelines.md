# Product Guidelines

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Primary Tone:** Encouraging & Empathetic. We celebrate the learning journey, understanding that acquiring new skills takes courage and effort.
- **Secondary Tone:** Professional & Clear. When dealing with account details, payments (credits), or scheduling, we prioritize clarity and trust.
- **Supportive:** We are the user's biggest cheerleader. Messages should be framed positively (e.g., "You're on your way!" instead of "Incomplete profile").
- **Inclusive:** Language should be welcoming to all skill levels and backgrounds.

## Visual Identity Principles
- **Clean & Accessible:** The interface should be clutter-free to focus on the content (video, chat, whiteboard). High contrast and clear typography are essential for accessibility.
- **Vibrant & Energetic:** Use a color palette that inspires creativity and action, reflecting the dynamic nature of skill swapping.
- **Human-Centric:** Use imagery of real people interacting, learning, and collaborating. Avoid generic stock illustrations where possible.

## User Experience (UX) Philosophy
- **Frictionless Connection:** The path from finding a match to starting a video call should be as short as possible.
- **Trust & Safety First:** Verified profiles and transparent review systems are central to the design to build community trust.
- **Reward Progress:** Visually celebrate milestones (first lesson taught, 10th hour learned) to reinforce the gamification elements.
