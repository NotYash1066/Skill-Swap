# Initial Concept
A peer-to-peer skill exchange platform where users can teach and learn skills from each other through real-time video calls, chat, and collaborative tools.

# Product Guide

## Vision
Skill-Swap aims to democratize education by creating a global community where knowledge is the currency. We envision a world where anyone can teach what they know and learn what they desire, fostering a culture of continuous, collaborative growth.

## Target Audience
- **Professionals:** Seeking to exchange niche technical expertise and expand their professional networks.
- **Students & Lifelong Learners:** Looking for affordable, personalized, and accessible peer-to-peer educational opportunities.
- **Hobbyists:** Passionate about sharing and acquiring creative or practical skills like cooking, music, or arts.

## Key Features & Unique Selling Points
- **Time Banking System:** A fair exchange economy where users earn credits by teaching and spend them to learn, ensuring a balanced and reciprocal community.
- **Gamification:** Engaging elements such as badges, leaderboards, and learning streaks to motivate users and encourage consistent participation.
- **AI-Driven Matching:** Intelligent algorithms that pair users based not just on skills, but also on learning styles, availability, and compatibility scores.
- **Core Real-Time Interaction:** High-quality video calling, instant messaging, and collaborative whiteboards to facilitate effective remote teaching.

## High-Level Goals (3-6 Months)
- **Platform Stability:** Ensure a seamless and low-latency experience for video calls and real-time collaboration.
- **Community Growth:** Focus on user retention and building a vibrant, active community of learners and teachers.
- **Scalability:** optimize the backend infrastructure to reliably support a growing user base and concurrent sessions.
